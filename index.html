04 — Principles

04 · What it's for

Participation without surrender. The defenses exist so that ordinary life doesn't depend on being exploitable.

  • Recognized without becoming a target.
  • Connected without becoming exposed.
  • Supported without becoming disposable.
  • Represented without being reduced.
A

Defend

Find the change nobody agreed to (between what a word seems to say and what it carries, what you see and what gets sent, who you are and the profile built of you) and expose it before the harm arrives.

B

Construct

Build better structure in its place: instruments for seeing systems, ways to meet people, games that teach a geometry. Only structure people can understand, question and refuse.

C

Label every claim

Say plainly what each piece of work is, what supports it and where it applies. Every new name comes with a definition grounded in established terms.

D

Open to challenge

Any framework here can be contested without first accepting its terms. Send objections to b@twl.today; substantive challenges and corrections are published, dated, beside the work they concern.

The site practices what it claims: no trackers, no third-party requests, hand-written HTML, source open on GitHub.
