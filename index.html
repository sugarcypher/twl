The lab is one person.

Every product, paper and instrument on this site was conceived, named, designed, built and launched by Briar Morgan Greenway — with AI as the lab bench, not the author.

Briar is a creative technologist and strategist who has spent more than two decades moving people and organisations from ambiguous problems to concrete systems — in consulting since 2001, and as an operator turning around businesses, building brands and growing communities. ThinkWell Labs, founded in 2024, is where that practice became a research program: start from an unconventional question, then carry it the whole way — concept, naming, brand, experience, technology, launch.

04 — Principles

04 · How the lab works

Upstream, not downstream. The site is the thesis it states.

A

Defend

Expose cognitive operators, strip invisible markup, intercept surveillance, confound adversarial reconnaissance — before the harm arrives.

B

Construct

Build beneficial structure in its place: instruments for seeing systems, new ways to meet people, games that teach a geometry.

C

Prove it structurally

Commitments enforced by architecture, not promised in a policy: zero trackers, zero third-party requests, hand-written HTML, source open on GitHub.
